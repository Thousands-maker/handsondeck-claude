    Optional

    ✦ Supercharged

    Adds an AI model that runs on this device. It helps with the parts rules can't do well, and still works with no internet once it's downloaded. Choose the size that suits your device.

    What it adds

    • Smarter sorting. Reads each sentence you write and decides which slide it belongs on.
    • Tighter headlines. Shortens long headlines to ten words or fewer, keeping every number you wrote.

    What it won't do

    • Write your deck for you, or add facts you didn't give it.
    • Match a cloud AI — it runs on your device, so it can misjudge.
    • Hide what it changed — anything it rewords is marked ✦ so you can check it.

        How sending for editing works

        Send for editing saves one HTML file that carries its own editor and its slide plan. Whoever you send it to edits it with nothing installed, saves, and sends it back. Open a deck brings their version in here with the structure intact. It's a file rather than a link on purpose: a link needs a host, and that is the moment content leaves the building.
